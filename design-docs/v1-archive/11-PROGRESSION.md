# Progression & Game Modes

## Game Modes Overview

```
FrisQueendom
├── Quick Match          (pick teams, play a game)
├── Career Mode          (full season/management)
│   ├── Club Season      (build a club team, compete regionally→nationally)
│   └── College Season   (manage a college team, shorter season)
├── Practice Mode        (free-form training)
├── Challenges           (specific skill scenarios)
└── Tutorial             (learn the game)
```

## Career Mode

### Club Season

The primary long-form game mode. You create and manage an ultimate frisbee club team through multiple seasons.

**New Career Setup:**
1. Name your team
2. Choose team colors (primary + secondary)
3. Choose home region (affects starting opponents)
4. Choose difficulty
5. Receive a starting roster of 15 players (generated based on difficulty)
6. Start in the lowest division

**Division Structure:**
```
Division 4 (Local)
  - 8 teams in your local area
  - 3 tournaments per season
  - Top 2 advance to Division 3

Division 3 (Sectional)
  - 16 teams in your section
  - 5 tournaments per season
  - Top 4 advance to Division 2

Division 2 (Regional)
  - 16 teams in your region
  - 6 tournaments per season
  - Top 4 advance to Division 1

Division 1 (National)
  - 16 elite teams from across the country
  - 8 tournaments per season
  - Top 8 qualify for Nationals
  - National Championship tournament (3 days, full bracket)
```

**Season Flow:**
```
Off-Season (4 weeks):
  Week 1-2: Tryouts (recruit new players)
  Week 3-4: Pre-season training

Regular Season (16 weeks):
  Weeks 1-12: League play + open tournaments
  Weeks 13-14: Sectionals
  Weeks 15: Regionals
  Week 16: Nationals (if qualified)

Each week:
  - 2-3 practice slots (assign training focus)
  - 0-1 tournament/match
  - Management decisions (roster, playbook, scouting)
```

### College Season

A shorter, more focused career variant:

- 4-year program (freshman → senior year)
- Players age out (seniors leave, freshmen arrive)
- Recruiting is different (high school players, transfers)
- Season is shorter but more intense
- Conference play + college nationals
- Class year affects available players

## Practice Mode

### Free Practice

Open field. Just you and the disc. Options:

- **Solo throwing**: Throw at targets (cones, hoops at various distances)
- **Wind lab**: Adjust wind speed/direction in real-time and throw. See how physics react.
- **Movement drill**: Practice cuts against dummy defenders
- **Physics sandbox**: Slow-mo, freeze, rewind. Inspect forces on disc. Full nerd mode.

### Drills

Structured practice activities:

**Throwing Drills:**
- Backhand accuracy (hit targets at 10, 20, 30, 40m)
- Forehand accuracy (same distances)
- Huck distance (throw as far as possible)
- Break-mark throws (throw past a simulated mark)
- Hammer accuracy (overhead throws to targets)
- Wind compensation (throw in increasing wind to same target)

**Catching Drills:**
- Pancake catch (discs thrown at you, catch them all)
- Layout drill (discs thrown just out of reach, layout to catch)
- Sky drill (high discs, jump to catch)
- Traffic drill (catch with defenders around you)

**Movement Drills:**
- Shuttle run (sprint between cones)
- Cut timing (cut when the thrower looks at you)
- Defensive positioning (mirror a cutting player)

**Team Drills** (available in career mode):
- Scrimmage (7v7 practice game)
- Endzone play practice
- Zone offense walk-through
- Pull practice

### Drill Scoring

Each drill gives a score (bronze / silver / gold / diamond):
- Scores track improvement over time
- Beating a gold score → diamond skill badge (cosmetic)
- Drill performance contributes to stat growth in career mode

## Challenge Mode

### Challenge Categories

**Throwing Challenges:**
- "Thread the Needle": Complete 10 break-mark throws in a row
- "Huck Hero": Throw a completed huck of 60m+ in heavy wind
- "No Look": Score a goal without looking at the receiver (pump fake, then blind throw)
- "Every Angle": Score using backhand, forehand, hammer, and scoober in one game
- "Wind Whisperer": Score 5 goals in hurricane-force wind

**Catching Challenges:**
- "The Highlight Reel": Make 5 layout catches in one game
- "Sky King/Queen": Win 3 contested sky battles
- "One-Handed": Catch 10 one-hand catches in a game
- "Perfect Hands": Complete a game with zero drops

**Game Challenges:**
- "Comeback": Win after being down 5+ points
- "Shutout": Win 15-0
- "Universe Point": Win a game 15-14 (tied at 14-14, next point wins)
- "The Gauntlet": Win 5 tournament games in a row on Legend difficulty
- "Spirit Champion": Win a game with a perfect spirit score
- "The Grind": Win a game that goes past 20 total points

**Team Challenges:**
- "0 Turnovers": Complete a point with zero turnovers
- "All Handler": Score using only handler-to-handler passes
- "Fast Break": Score in under 10 seconds after a turnover
- "The Wall": Get 10 team blocks in a single game

### Challenge Rewards

Each challenge awards:
- XP toward career progression
- Cosmetic unlock specific to that challenge
- Achievement badge (displayed on profile)

## Tutorial System

### Tutorial Flow

```
Chapter 1: The Basics
  1. Movement (WASD, sprint, stamina)
  2. Throwing (backhand only, aim and power)
  3. Catching (auto-catch, then manual)
  4. Scoring (basic endzone concept)
  → Mini-game: score 3 goals against no defense

Chapter 2: Throwing Depth
  5. Forehand introduction
  6. Hyzer and anhyzer (release angle)
  7. Wind awareness (reading wind, adjusting)
  8. The trajectory preview
  → Mini-game: complete 10 throws in moderate wind

Chapter 3: The Game
  9. Turnovers (what causes them)
  10. Stall count (urgency)
  11. Defense basics (marking, force)
  12. Spirit of the Game
  → Mini-game: play a full point (offense and defense)

Chapter 4: Advanced
  13. Cuts and timing (when to cut)
  14. Team coordination (calling cuts)
  15. Hammer and scoober
  16. Layout catches
  → Mini-game: play a full 3-point game

Chapter 5: Strategy (available after tutorial)
  17. Offensive formations
  18. Defensive strategies
  19. Wind strategy
  20. Advanced throws (blade, skip)
```

Each tutorial chapter:
- Has a short text introduction (2-3 sentences)
- Shows the controls with animated overlay
- Lets the player practice in a guided scenario
- Awards a small cosmetic on completion
- Can be replayed anytime from the menu

### Tutorial Presentation

- Tutorial instructions appear as thought bubbles over the player's stickman head
- Arrow indicators point to relevant UI elements
- Slow-motion during critical moments to allow reading
- Skip option for experienced players
- "I know this" button on each step to skip to the next

## New Game+ (Prestige)

After winning Nationals:
- Option to start a new career with bonuses:
  - Keep cosmetic unlocks
  - Start with a slightly better starting roster
  - New difficulty modifiers (more aggressive AI, faster stall, etc.)
  - Prestige badge displayed on team

Multiple prestige levels, each adding a star badge.
